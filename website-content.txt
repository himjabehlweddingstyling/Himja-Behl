FINAL ABOUT COPY
I started wedding styling because I realised how overwhelming getting dressed for an Indian wedding can become. There are hundreds of designers, thousands of references, multiple functions and an endless stream of opinions. Somewhere between all those choices, it’s surprisingly easy to lose sight of the person actually wearing the clothes.
My approach starts there. Before I recommend an outfit, I want to understand you — your personal style, your body, the colours you feel beautiful in, the wedding you’re creating and how you want to feel when you walk into each celebration. From there, I build a wardrobe where every look has its own identity, but everything still feels connected.
And I don’t look at a wedding in isolation. A bride’s look exists alongside the groom, her mother, his mother, siblings and the people standing beside them. When I’m styling a family, I think about that entire visual story — making sure everyone feels individual without the wedding looking overly coordinated or contrived.
My job is to make the process feel lighter. To filter the noise, find what is worth considering, tell you when something isn’t working, and obsess over the details that eventually make a look feel effortless — the silhouette, jewellery, drape, shoes, hair, accessories and everything in between.

Journey 1 — A Legacy, Restyled
Client
	Tushar Behl — Groom & Venture Capitalist
	Event context
	Wedding · Delhi — A time-strapped groom balancing an intense work and travel schedule in the run-up to his wedding.
	Introduction
	With work and constant travel leaving very little time for wedding shopping, Tushar needed a process that was clear, efficient and easy to visualise. The aim was to create a groom wardrobe that felt polished and contemporary without making styling another demanding part of wedding planning. 
	The Brief
	Build a complete groom wardrobe around his personal style while keeping shopping focused and time-efficient. Alongside the new looks, there was one sentimental challenge: reimagine his father’s 30-year-old wedding sherwani for his own wedding day. His eyewear also needed to feel intentional rather than something that disrupted the finished looks.
	The Process
	The process began with detailed discovery conversations to understand what Tushar would genuinely feel comfortable wearing. Before the first shopping sprint, a curated lookbook gave him a realistic visual direction for each look. Shopping was then narrowed to a considered selection of designers and options — limited enough to save time, but varied enough to make confident choices. His father’s sherwani was altered, revived and completely restyled with jewellery and a saffron-toned safa to create a modern groom look while preserving its family history. Custom eyewear was also developed for each function so that his glasses became part of the styling rather than an afterthought.
	The Result
	A wardrobe that felt effortless, personal and distinctly his own. Every shopping decision became easier to navigate, a 30-year-old family sherwani was given a contemporary second life, and even his eyewear became an integral part of the final styling. The result was a modern groom wardrobe with a strong sense of family and history running through it.
	Client Testimonial
	“The overall look came together as a nice modern groom while still having the family vibe to it.”

	6. The Styling Process
FINAL WEBSITE COPY
01 — UNDERSTAND
We begin with you: your wedding, functions, personal style, proportions, colour preferences, budget and the way you want to feel. The goal is clarity before we look at a single outfit.
02 — BUILD THE DIRECTION
I shape the visual language of your wardrobe — silhouettes, colours, designers, references and how each function should feel. If I’m styling more than one family member, this is also where the larger wedding story begins to come together.
03 — CURATE & SHOP
The search becomes focused. I shortlist, source and edit the options worth your time, through virtual curation or in-person shopping. We refine outfits, customisation, fittings and selections until the wardrobe feels right — not simply finished.
04 — STYLE HEAD TO TOE
Once the clothes are locked, we complete the look: jewellery, shoes, drapes, accessories, hair and makeup direction, and the small styling decisions that change how an outfit finally reads. You walk into the wedding knowing the entire look has been considered.
